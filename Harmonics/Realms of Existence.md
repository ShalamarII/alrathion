Note:
The lower the level, the closer to "nothingness"

---

Level 0: Vraul Itself
At this realm of existence, there are only 2 things: Vraul & Interactors. Nobody exists at this level but a person can emulate an interactor (Virtuosos when acting as a conduit, phylactery, etc.)

Level 1: Forces & Application
This level is where vrail finds itself actually interacting with those Interactors. This gets you Notes (Heat, Light, etc.) from these basic interactions, and laments come from complex interactions.

Level 2: Physicality & The Body
This is where the body lies, of not only humanoid creatures but of all things. Its machinations are defined by those forces that interact, and though seemingly emergent, these beings/bodies are not unique in their actions. (Humankind irl rn)

Level 3: Consciousness & Magic
This is the place which psionics and consciousness came to exist. People existed first with instinct, then their interactions with magic created consciousness. ("more perfected consciousness")

Level 4: Transcendence
This is the level under which consciousness truly leaves the body and can stand on its own, remaining despite its natural Vraul interacting with all others. Its is affected as a state of thought, not having instinct, reaching "True Silence".
