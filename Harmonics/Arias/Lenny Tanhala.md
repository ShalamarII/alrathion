

### Sound Dampening
|                   | Description                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2-3)** | The surroundings are quieted, muffling sound that would normally be easily heard within a 5 yd radius. Gain a +1 to Hearing and Stealth Rolls for the next 10 Minutes (up to +3). |
| **Level 2 (4-5)** | Silence brings you comfort. Gain a +2 to your next roll.                                                                                                                          |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | The minds of those around you are silenced. Deal 1d4-2 damage to each enemy affected by your spell.                                                                               |

### Sound Amplification
|                   | Description                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Level 1 (2-3)** | Your surroundings become louder, making it hard to think. Apply a -2 to all Speech checks in a 10yd radius and a +2 to Hearing checks.                             |
| **Level 2 (4-5)** | Sound begins to cause chaos around you, imbuing your spell with psychic properties. An enemy affected by your spell must succeed a Will check or become Nauseated. |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | A cacophony of sound rings in the ears of those affected by your spell. Any affected enemy takes 1d6 damage.                                                       |

### Sound Mimicry
|                   | Description                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2-3)** | You find yourself reaching into the mind of those around you, humming an Aria of their own. You roll on another selected being's Aria table. |
| **Level 2 (4-5)** | Enraptured with their aria, you progress the melody. Upgrade the Aria to Level 2.                                                            |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | You resolve the aria, releasing its full effects. Upgrade the Aria to Level 3.                                                               |

### Sound Duplication
|                   | Description                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2-3)** | When speaking, your speech echoes forcefully, with a deep timbre. +2 to fright checks.                                                                                                         |
| **Level 2 (4-5)** | All sounds begin to echo, regardless of the location. People within a 5yd radius of your spell (or the first target) must succeed a Hearing check or suffer a -2 to DX and IQ for 1d6-3 turns. |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | Even the brainwaves are duplicated, causing inability to think. Targets must succeed on a Will roll or gain the Blind effect for 1d6-4 turns.                                                  |

### Sound Distortion
|                   | **Description**                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | Your Umbra distorts an enemy's equilibrium. When that enemy next activates their equilibrium, it is downgraded one level.                                             |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | Your Umbra distorts an ally's equilibrium. When that ally next activates an Aria (in combat), it is upgraded one level. (This does not stack.)                        |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | You and your Umbra sync, distorting the equilibrium in an area. Within a 3 yd radius, any enemies receive the level 1 debuff and all allies receive the level 2 buff. |

### Music Manipulation
|                  | Description                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ## Level 1 (2-3) | You take harmony and rise it an octave. Whenever you next cast a spell, you receive a -1 yd to effective range.                                           |
| ## Level 2 (4-5) | You reduce the dissonance in the negative space. You share this bonus with all allies for 5 turns.                                                        |
| ## Level 3 (6+)  | You make other's spells an aria of your own. Choose an spell from an allies spell-knowledge. You may cast that spell once. You know the spell for 1 week. |
