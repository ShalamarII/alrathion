
#### Condensation Manipulation
| Dice Roll | Aria Style             | Short Description                                             |
| --------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1         | Water Defense          | Creating watery shields, that can absorb attacks              |
| 2         | Water Generation       | Ability to make targets wet, which will apply affects to them |
| 3         | Viscosity Manipulation | Make things thicker and harder to move through                |
| 4         | Steam Manipulation     | Create hot steamy steam                                       |
| 5         | Healing Water          | Healing People through water                                  |
| 6         | Water Bestowal         | Allying those around you with water                           |

#### Water Barrier (1)
| **Level**         | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | You conjure up a faint, shimmering film of water over your skin. Gain +1 to DR.                                                                               |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | You pull the water from you, forcing it forward into a shield of ice. Create a shield that has 3 HP and blocks any non-ephemeral attacks.                     |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | You may increase the density of the ice until it bursts. Anyone within a 3-yard cone from the front of your shield must roll a DX–2 save or take 2d+2 damage. |

#### Water Generation (2)
| **Level**        | **Effect** |
|------------------|------------|
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | You pull water from within the atmosphere onto a target. When you do so, you apply the *Wet* effect onto someone (–1 to DX per stack, 1d–3 damage if hit with cold/ice-based attacks). |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | You link up to 3 enemies that are within 5 yards of one another. Whenever you apply *Wet* onto one of them, you apply it on all 3. |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | You freeze your link with your enemies. Any enemy hit because of this must succeed a Will–2 roll or be frozen in ice for 1d6–3 turns. |

#### Viscosity Manipulation (3)
| **Level**        | **Effect** |
|------------------|------------|
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | You squeeze your hand, thickening the liquids in your surroundings. For 10 turns, any enemy hit with a liquid within 5 yards takes an additional 1 damage. |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | You begin to thicken the blood inside your enemy's head. An enemy that receives a liquid attack must also roll a DX–2 save or have their Move halved (duration: 3 turns). |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | You increase the pressure, pushing against the enemy's skull. An enemy dealt more than 1/5 of their HP and affected by a liquid attack within the last 10 turns must roll against HT or enter knockdown. |

#### Steam Manipulation (4)
| **Level**         | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | Steam rises from your body. Gain +1 to all checks for 5 turns.                                                                                                          |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | With your call, the earth becomes a steam geyser. You cause a pocket of steam to escape the earth on 1 hex within 5 hexes. Any enemy within that hex takes 1d–1 damage. |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | Steam proliferates on the battlefield, adding another tear and upgrading the damage to 1d+1.                                                                            |

#### Healing Salve (5)
| **Level**         | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | Water begins to cure your wounds. Begin rapidly healing 1 per turn for 1d6–2 turns. (This does not affect injury damage.)                                                                        |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | You may touch an ally and apply the healing buff to them as well.                                                                                                                                |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | You may increase the number of targets to 3 within a range of 5 yards.                                                                                                                           |

#### Water Bestowal (6)
| **Level**        | **Effect** |
|------------------|------------|
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | Your Umbra pipes up, waiting for your command. You may attach your Umbra to an ally within 3 yards or yourself as a free action, granting them +1 to ST or IQ. This state lasts 1 hour. |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | Your Umbra makes movement easier, allowing fluidity that seems inhuman. This grants an additional +1 DX to the user. |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | Your Umbra can synchronize with the dissonance within the user's heart. The user may cast a spell using the water elemental's mana (3 mana). When the elemental loses all mana, the effect ends. |
