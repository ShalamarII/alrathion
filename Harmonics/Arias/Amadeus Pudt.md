### Electrokinetic Surfing (1)

|                   | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | You manifest a field of electricity underneath your feet, creating a buffer between you and the ground. You may act as if you have the Terrain Adaptation (B93) advantage for 10 turns.                                         |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | You bond directly with your field, creating a ride-able vehicle. It allows you to move double your move for 10 turns.                                                                                                           |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | You control your electricity in such a way that the board stays manifested. The duration of the board is increased to 1 hour, and can be used by any person who you deem an ally. For the ally, it adds your move to their own. |

### Electric Enhancement (2)

|                   | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | You enhance yourself with your own electricity, allowing you to move farther than you would normally. Alongside your normal maneuver, you may take an additional step.                                                                      |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | You directly activate the nerves in your body, increasing your reaction time. You directly gain the Enhanced Time Sense advantage for 3 turns.                                                                                              |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | Your enhancement increases your destructive capabilities. Any attacks you make for 1d turn(s) contain an electric element to them, dealing an additional 1d damage and causing an IQ roll or else an enemy receives the Mental Stun effect. |

### Lightning Projection (3)

|                   | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | You call your Umbra to your targets. When a spell successfully hits a target (even if they resist the spell), the Umbra reverses their polarity. For 1d–3 turns, they will be struck by lightning for 1 damage. |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | Your Umbra manifests a cloud of lightning above them. Upgrade the damage to 1d–3, and the target gains 1 level of Shock.                                                                                        |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | Your Umbra may transfer to those nearby after their target is dead, resetting the timer.                                                                                                                        |

### Lightning Aura (4)

|                   | **Effect**                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | You project an aura of lightning from yourself. Any enemy within your vicinity (2 yd × SM) takes 1d–3 damage.          |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | The aura can be controlled. You may move the aura onto any ally within 15 yds.                                         |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | The aura is now self-sufficient. You may move the aura anywhere with its move (5 yd). The damage it deals is now 1d+2. |
### Bio-Electric Kinesis (5)

|                   | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | You gain the ability to manipulate your own body's latent electricity. You reduce the time between thinking and casting by 1 second (reduce Time to Cast by 1 second, minimum 1). |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | You cause electricity to stimulate your heart, increasing blood flow and warmth. You heal faster, gaining 1 additional point per rest.                                            |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | The electricity begins to arc from your body, jumping from target to target. Anyone affected by a spell of yours must make a Will check or receive 2 levels of Shock.             |
### Lightning Discharge (6)

|                   | **Effect**                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1 (2–3)** | Electricity flies from your body, linking you and any allies within 5 yds. Any ally that takes the Wait maneuver may act in response to something happening to you. |
| **Level 2 (4–5)** | The link affects enemies. Whenever an enemy passes through the link (once per turn), they must pass an IQ roll or you gain 1 level of Aim on them.                  |
| **Level 3 (6+)**  | The electricity links your very thoughts to your allies. Anyone linked with you receives the benefit of your Aim/Evaluate maneuvers.                                |
