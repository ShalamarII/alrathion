*An aria is a song of your own, the melody in the back of your mind. To share an aria is to share one's deepest self, the mind we do not know.*
<span class="r-align">- Professor Windgale</span>

Secondary Effects (named Arias) have 3 levels which are based on amplitude of the spell. At level 1 you see small, trifling effects created around the caster or around the spell. At level 2 you see something more impactful, that provides a minor benefit to the spell being cast. At level 3 the benefit is upgraded, effectively masking another spell within the first.

At every multiple of 2 (MoS), you manifest 1 level of Aria. Roll a d6 on your sheet to see of what type.

| Dice Roll | Aria Style                           | Short Description                                                                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1         | Sound Dampening                      | These Arias have to do with eliminating or quieting sound                               |
| 2         | Sound Amplification                  | These Arias have to do with amplifying or increasing sound                              |
| 3         | Sound Mimicry                        | These Arias have to do with mimicking sounds (Copying another's Arias)                  |
| 4         | Sound Duplication (Aria Duplication) | These Arias have to do with duplicating sound (Dual-casting Arias)                      |
| 5         | Sound Distortion                     | These Arias have to do with distorting sound (Removing other's Arias or helping allies) |
| 6         | Music Manipulation                   | These Arias have to do with the manipulation of music (Spells themselves)               |

The surroundings are quieted, muffling sound that would normally be easily heard.
