Abilities/Spells are known as "Experiences". They are encoded knowledge and skills directly, and can be sold, traded and stolen.

A Hail Mary is an extraordinary attempt to do something out of your normal reach. 
Exertion Consequences are when doing a hail mary,

When you use a skill (Acrobatics, Strength, Herbology, etc.), you have a chance of encoding it into your personal experience (Self). This is based off of the complexity dice of the Skill you used. To gain a requisite level of knowledge in a skill, you must:
1. Succeed in using the skill (Roll against the difficulty score \[or two higher if doing a hail mary]).
2. Roll against the complexity dice of that skill and get a one.
This allows you to gain a level of familiarity with that Skill according to your General dice in that Skill. You may only attempt to gain Familiarity if you are within one Skill Dice of the new Skill.

You add your Generic modifier for the Skill to an ability roll (Mechanic = INT).

> Example 1
> If you gain familiarity with Mechanic (1d12 - INT) but only have a 1d8 in INT, you gain 1d8 familiarity, which may help with diagnosing a vehicle but you will not suddenly be a full blown mechanic.

To use an ability, you roll 3d6 against the Complexity Dice for that Skill, aiming to roll at or above than it.
