Dice should be a representation of chance, Cards should be a representation of outcome. The goal is to create a system that accurately feels more skillful without the "fail" floor that the randomness of dice can give. Realistically you can only be so bad at something, even if that thing is skilled and D&D often represents a 1 as "you are in such a unique position that you completely and utterly fail". This does not strike me as fair.
