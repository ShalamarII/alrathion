Parry/Block

- If you have proficiency with a weapon, you may parry an attack or physical spell. This causes you to take half the damage roll. If you have gear that enables block, take no damage.
- You may also attempt to dodge an attack, though it will make you \[unbalanced], which prevents you from using or concentrating on Major Abilities.
- Redirecting makes you take half damage and become \[unbalanced], but you also put the attacker in an unbalanced state.
