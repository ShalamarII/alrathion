Abilities describe the fantastical and tactical options a user has. They may be as simple as *Lacerate Flesh* or as complex as *Tongue of Babel*. Spells are the same, they only have the additional mana

##### Card Format:
There are two hexes on a card, the range and resource hex. Every ability uses a resource, even if that resource is "None" Range designates how the spell works (Cone, Line, One Target, Multiple Targets, 3 Hex Area).

Cards also have a description, a reference to the ability book (Page Num + dataID)

Abilities can be two-sided, having a Major/Minor distinction. Major versions are one-time use per day, while Minor versions have an infinite usage (provided you have the resources to cast/use them).


##### Types of Abilities:
"Passive" - Abilities that auto-activate based on certain circumstances or are "always on"
"Status" - Abilities that represent something that effects affects the environment or people  around you (Stunned, Difficult Terrain, Blinded, etc.) These abilities do not "belong to you" although they may have been caused by you.
"Active" - Abilities that activate based on you choosing them and paying a resource cost. This can include "always on" abilities that can be switched on/off.
