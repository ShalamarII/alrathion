Creating a character is about creating a story. You choose a gameplay style, craft a character that can have an arc, and ready yourselves to touch the world you are thrust upon.


#### Tales
Tales are a story of what you're character has been doing/is doing before this adventure you're planning to go on. Tales describe who you are. They define traits like what you're good at, who you may know, what you do for coin.

#### Fates
 No one knows which way your path leads, especially not you. But something has been written and your path has been set. Fates describe a motivation of yours, something you set out to finish, escape or become.

Fates Options
- Sins of the Damned - Something you've done is your past has haunted you; its' gaze can no longer be ignored.
