### Design Philosophy

^6d57b3

There are two types of generation that will be happening:
1. Basic Generation - Something that can be done on a whim for a GM.
2. Fine Generation - Something that a GM can do with a good amount of time (used for major places, or as needed)
Basic Generation is meant to be done for the ENTIRE WORLD at once when automated (and therefore must remain simple enough to be automated/understandable by a GM) and individually by a GM if they wish.
Fine Generation is meant to be done primarily through automation. It's better to build out two methods for receiving similar results (GM Friendly, Automation Friendly) than it is to try and make it fit to both directly. This means that the ***output*** needs to be GM readable (Even down to the JSON), but can still be automated fully.
