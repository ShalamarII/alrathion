Factions can control hexes fully, partially, or not at all. This is the level of [[Affiliation]] that a faction has with a hex, which is marked by the % of control a faction has. These numbers may not be indicative of the exact situation of the hex (A hex controlled by one faction but industries of that hex are controlled by another.)

Each day you get 3 actions (rolls) (Morning, Afternoon, Evening). The timescale a faction works on is 7am-7pm daily. These rolls represent your factions capacity to do certain tasks (Subterfuge, Work, Explore, Expedition, Build Reputation, Custom Actions). Upgrades to these actions can be bought using Gold.

### Espionage

The espionage action is about interacting with other factions in a non-forward way, whether that is making shady deals for information, planting a spy, or sabotaging a enemy factions supply line.

### Work

The work action is about doing tasks in exchange for money or items. This may be taking government contracts or quests, selling items, or even farming for crops to sell later. This is meant to be the most flexible action, excepting Custom Actions.

### Explore

The explore action is about gathering intelligence on hexes, with debuffs based on how far they are away from a faction-settled hex.
