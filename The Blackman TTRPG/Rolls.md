Skills:
- 3d6 vs the skill +/- HM

Unskilled Rolls:
- 3d6 vs the DM's choice of how difficult it should be (chart provided).

Skilled Contests:
- Each person rolls the skill dice +/- HM and person who higher wins.

Effect Contests:
- The person using the effect (Spell, Persuasion, etc.) rolls their dice for the skill. The influenced person rolls the stat specified.

Grimoire may contain any spell, but you must succeed on a skillful roll (Generic Magic with Source) to use it. If you fail, stamina/mana/equivalent is still used. If you succeed, your Effect Contest roll is replaced by the highest number on your skill dice. (e.g. if a spell is a d6, your Effect Contest roll is always a 6).
