# Subterranean Sanctum of the Tears 

*For many years the sanctum remained sealed. Currently it is badly infested by goblins. It is said that countless treasures of ancient books are stored here.*

1. Remains of a cleric, a brass key clutched in his hands.
2. A large pile of rubble hides a helm.
3. A basket with a strange, slightly glowing breastplate.
4. A round double door with a keyhole to the west.
