Affiliation is a representation of the control over the hex that a faction has.



| Affiliation Type             | Roll  |
| ---------------------------- | ----- |
| [Economic](economic_types)   | 3d8/3 |

Base is a 1d6 roll which represents the % * 10 of affiliation is controlled by the Political type.
