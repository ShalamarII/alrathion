## Emberleaf Stew

Ingredients: Fireroot tubers, sun-dried brimberries, smoked venison, wild onions, sea salt, elderwine  
Prep Time: 20 minutes  
Time to Cook: 2 hours  
Cooking Instructions:  
Peel and cube the fireroot tubers and simmer them in elderwine for 30 minutes. Add chopped wild onions and smoked venison. Stir in brimberries and a pinch of sea salt. Let simmer for an additional 90 minutes over a low flame until thick and fragrant.  
Author: Thalindra Moonglow

## Skyroot Goulash

Ingredients: Skyroot bulbs, black pepper moss, cavehare loin, whispervine oil, frost carrots, stone salt  
Prep Time: 15 minutes  
Time to Cook: 1 hour, 15 minutes  
Cooking Instructions:  
Dice the skyroot bulbs and frost carrots. Sear cavehare loin in whispervine oil until browned. Add vegetables and cook until softened. Sprinkle in black pepper moss and stone salt. Simmer with a splash of springwater for 45 minutes until the stew thickens and the aroma deepens.

## Stormscale Chowder

Ingredients: Stormscale fish, glimmer kelp, embercorn, thistle cream, dune garlic, cracked shellpepper  
Prep Time: 25 minutes  
Time to Cook: 1 hour  
Cooking Instructions:  
Fillet the stormscale fish and cube into small pieces. Sauté dune garlic and glimmer kelp in a deep pot. Stir in embercorn and cook for 10 minutes. Add fish and thistle cream. Season with cracked shellpepper and simmer until creamy and golden. Serve hot with crispbread.

## Dracoflame Consommé

Ingredients: Flameheart peppers, liquid ember sap, phoenix egg yolk, powdered obsidian salt, golden shallots, wyvern marrow broth  
Prep Time: 40 minutes  
Time to Cook: 6 hours  
Cooking Instructions:  
Slice flameheart peppers thinly and sear until their skins crackle. In a cauldron, combine with golden shallots and slowly pour in the wyvern marrow broth. Stir in phoenix yolk and simmer for 5 hours. At the last hour, add liquid ember sap and a dusting of obsidian salt. Strain until the liquid gleams ruby-red and nearly glows.  
Notes: Said to ignite the soul and heighten magical awareness. Consumed by archmages before duels.  
Author: *Dai Muldrane*

## Glimmerwheat Moonloaf

Ingredients: Glimmerwheat flour, silver dew yeast, fae honey, starfruit rind, dreammilk, crushed starlace petals  
Prep Time: 1 hour  
Time to Cook: 2 hours  
Cooking Instructions:  
Activate silver dew yeast in warmed dreammilk until it pulses with light. Mix in glimmerwheat, fae honey, and grated starfruit rind. Fold the dough seven times while whispering the *Lunar Chant of Proofing*. Sprinkle with starlace petals and bake until the crust shimmers.  
Notes: Traditionally gifted to oracles; rumored to grant lucid dreams of the future.  
Author: *Dai Muldrane*

## Ashen Elk Tartare

Ingredients: Charred elk loin, midnight truffle shavings, frostleaf oil, black salt, whispermint, cured ghostcap mushrooms

Time to Cook: None (served raw)

## Evershade Elixir

Ingredients: Nightroot essence, shadow orchid petals, glacial springwater, powdered moonstone, whispergrass extract, basilisk tear  
Prep Time: 2 hours (must be brewed under twilight)  
Time to Cook: 3 days of slow infusion  
Cooking Instructions:  
In a crystal decanter, layer nightroot essence with whispergrass extract. Slowly pour in glacial springwater and stir clockwise with a bone wand. Add shadow orchid petals and moonstone. Let steep under starlight for three nights. Add one drop of basilisk tear just before serving.  
Notes: Banned in three kingdoms for its use in memory alchemy. Said to allow the drinker to relive their most cherished memory—once.  
Author: *Dai Muldrane*

## Moltenroot Risotto

Ingredients: Ashroot rice, lava truffle slivers, moon cream, ember broth, sun-chive stalks, sylvan pepper  
Prep Time: 30 minutes  
Time to Cook: 45 minutes  
Cooking Instructions:  
Sauté chopped sun-chives in moon cream until translucent. Stir in ashroot rice and slowly ladle in warm ember broth, one cup at a time, stirring constantly. Add lava truffle slivers at the halfway mark. Finish with a final swirl of moon cream and a pinch of sylvan pepper.  
Author: *Dai Muldrane*

## Braised Griffon Shank

## Sunspire Curry

Ingredients: Sunspire root paste, krakenmilk, ghost lime, fire shrimp, basil emberleaf, moonrice  
Prep Time: 25 minutes  
Time to Cook: 40 minutes  
Cooking Instructions:  
Fry sunspire paste in oil until fragrant. Add krakenmilk and bring to a simmer. Toss in peeled fire shrimp, chopped ghost lime rind, and emberleaf. Simmer until shrimp are curled and vibrant. Serve over steamed moonrice.
