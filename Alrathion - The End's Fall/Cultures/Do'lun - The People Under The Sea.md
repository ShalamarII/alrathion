Do-Loon

The Do'lun are a beautiful and radiant race. They live underneath the sea, with a prior establishment in Valenfir. Their people largely fell during The Great War.

###  Underneath The Sea
Do'lunian people have gills, with naturally iridescent skin (Often shades of blue and green.) They value honor, justice, and freedom. 

### The Costilian Origins
Do'lunian people have ancestral ties directly to the [[The People from Beyond the Stars - Cosmillae|Cosmilla People]]. They are vaguely aware of this and as such deny all religious beings but the Cosmillae. 

### Lack of Reproduction
The Do'lun cannot reproduce, except through other races. This brings them to steal people once every few decades. They then carry the Do'lun's people, borne of the imagination of those "carrying them". For this reason, they have myriad looks among themselves.
