Universe hopping people that act as intergalactic magic brokers. They have rounded tendrils that they "walk" on. Their ship is made of semi-organic matter called Tuion (Toy-on)

### Dimensional Travel
The Cosmillae are a universe hopping people that broker control over magical information. One can summon them through a process known as the Calling, where the people in question transmit Vraul (look of device different based off civilization) to the Cosmillae to pay the trip's fare.

### The Calling
Many ancient rituals lost to time were a call for the Cosmillae. They must put out a significant amount of Vraul, usually expended from a power stone. Then, the Cosmillae lock onto the signal and begin consuming it. As they consume it, the Tuion becomes flexible, similar to a liquid metal until it becomes fully liquid. Then, the ship is warped by the space around it through a localized black hole.

### The Great Book
The Great Book is a record of all information the Cosmillae know and trade. The only thing that can cause information to be struck from the book is to pay a high, high price. The book acts similar to a digital system, having a search function, gestures and "exports". The Exports are direct memories, which can be version controlled (User cannot speak about this information, User does not remember who gave the information to them, User forfeits a memory). In this way, they are the Universe's brokers.
