### General Information
***Full Name***: Waterspine Cattail
***Scientific Name***: Tempus Orinai
***Classification***: Bulb
### Traits
***Taste***: Has a decadent taste, adding a depth that is subtle, but can be magnified with spice.
***Color:*** It is a light blue, similar to beach water. 
***Looks***: It is translucent, as the holding back water. The bulb is thick, with the bulb having slime on the inside.
***Smell***: It smells like fresh water, refreshing the senses and clearing up the sinuses a bit.
***Best Cooking Method***: The best way to cook it is to first cut it at the bulb's stem, then perforate it and push the fluid out. Then, roll the leaves tightly and cut them into a spiral pattern. You can then dry it out and use it as a garnish or fry it, in which case the skin will "bloom".
### Description:
The Waterspine Cattail is a rare plant that grows in oasis like areas. It is light blue with a translucent film covering the liquid inside. It has a stem that looks withered with a bulb of liquid that is barely standing up. Once the stem is detached, the bulb becomes purple and a light neurotoxin is released into the liquid. It is often used in Idran cuisine as a garnish (dried). The skin is also fried and used as a "chip" for dips.

***Effects when ingested***:
if ingested without proper preparation, it will cause a headache and sluggishness. Otherwise, it hydrates the body extremely well, with the liquid being a more "dense" water.
##### Fun Fact: 
The water inside the bulb is actually an amazing lotion, but it causes the skin to become translucent.

Medical Uses: translucent skin gives you an easier time seeing wounds, incisions, etc.
